# Roadmap

This project will stay practical: every future feature should help a law student or researcher write, verify, revise, or submit a paper with less friction and less risk of hallucinated legal authority.

## v1.3.0: Stronger Verification

- Add source-date fields to journal and formatting tables.
- Build a legal verification checklist template that can be copied into papers or Zotero notes.
- Add more examples for statute, case, and literature verification.

## v1.4.0: More Subfield Prompts

- Add prompt packs for civil law, criminal law, administrative law, economic law, international law, jurisprudence, procedural law, and intellectual property law.
- For each subfield, include typical research questions, argument structures, common evidence types, and reviewer objections.

## v1.5.0: Word and Submission Workflow

- Improve the Word document workflow with more journal-specific checklists.
- Add a thesis-format preparation checklist for Chinese law schools.
- Add examples for revision letters and responses to reviewers.

## v1.6.0: Citation Tooling

- Expand `citation-converter.py` test coverage.
- Add safer warnings for unrecognized citation formats.
- Consider optional integrations with Zotero export formats, without making Zotero mandatory.

## Long-Term Direction

- Keep all legal and journal information source-aware and date-aware.
- Prefer copyable Markdown workflows over complex software dependencies.
- Avoid turning the project into a legal advice tool.
